Enrico Daga is a Senior Research Fellow working in knowledge engineering and the Semantic Web, at the intersection of data integration, knowledge graphs, and data management. He is the co-creator of [SPARQL Anything](https://sparql-anything.cc), a framework that lets any data format be queried as if it were RDF through the façade pattern. He has extensive experience with knowledge graph construction and its application across numerous domains, including cultural heritage, smart cities, teaching and learning, and social sciences.

He co-chairs the [W3C Data Façades Community Group](https://www.w3.org/groups/cg/facade-x/) and is Area Editor of the *Journal of Web Semantics*. He is Visiting Professor at the Università di Torino (2024–2026) and has supervised five PhD students to completion. He holds a PhD in Computer Science from The Open University (2018); before joining the OU in 2013, he worked on Web Semantics at the Italian National Research Council (CNR).
