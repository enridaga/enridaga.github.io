## Knowledge Graph Engineering

Design, construction, and curation of knowledge graphs at scale — including the development of reusable ontologies, modular graph patterns, and engineering pipelines. Flagship output: **SPARQL Anything**, a façade-based framework enabling SPARQL queries over heterogeneous data sources without custom ETL code.

## Data Life-cycles in Distributed Infrastructures

How data is created, shared, transformed, and retired across distributed systems — with attention to provenance, quality, and interoperability. Applied in the context of institutional open data (OU Open Knowledge Graph) and cross-organisational cultural heritage platforms.

## Computational Policies

Machine-readable representations of licences, digital rights, and access conditions, enabling automated compliance reasoning in data pipelines and knowledge graph applications.

## Application Domains

- **Cultural Heritage** — musical heritage (Polifonia), museum collections (SPICE), listening history (LED)
- **Smart Cities** — urban data integration, mobility, citizen sensing (MK:Smart, MK:5G, ClimateSense)
- **Healthcare** — knowledge-driven clinical decision support and data integration
- **Digital Humanities** — computational approaches to humanities scholarship, AI and music
