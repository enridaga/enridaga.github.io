Enrico Daga is a Senior Research Fellow at the [Knowledge Media Institute (KMi)](https://kmi.open.ac.uk), STEM Faculty, The Open University, where he has worked since 2013. He holds a PhD from The Open University (2018) and a BA from Università RomaTRE, Rome (2004).

Before joining The Open University, Enrico worked at the Italian National Research Council (CNR) from 2005 to 2013, contributing to foundational work in semantic content management and knowledge infrastructure.

His research sits at the intersection of **Knowledge Graphs**, **Semantic Web technologies**, and their application to real-world challenges in Cultural Heritage, Smart Cities, Healthcare, and the Humanities. He is the principal investigator and Technical Director of the [Polifonia](http://www.polifonia-project.eu) project (EU H2020) — a flagship effort to build a knowledge graph network for European musical heritage — and co-investigator on the ongoing [ClimateSense](https://climateSense.eu) (CHIST-ERA/EPSRC) and [MultiPoD](https://multipod.eu) (Horizon Europe) projects.

Since 2025, Enrico serves as **Chair of the W3C Data Façades Community Group**, and has been **Co-chair of the DARIAH AI and Music (AIM) working group** since 2019. He is also **Visiting Professor** at the Università di Torino (2024–2026), where he teaches a Master-level course on Knowledge Modelling for the Semantic Web.

Enrico is the creator of **[SPARQL Anything](https://sparql.anything.cc)**, a widely-used open-source tool that enables querying of heterogeneous data formats using SPARQL, and has served as Director of the [OU Open Knowledge Graph](https://data.open.ac.uk) platform since 2010.

He is an Area Editor for the *Journal of Web Semantics* (Elsevier, 2023–present) and has served on the programme committees of ISWC, ESWC, ECAI, TheWebConf, EKAW, and many others.
